You are protected. Speaking up is safe.

The Firm has a strict anti-retaliation policy. Coming forward — whether or not your concern is ultimately investigated — cannot and will not affect your employment, standing, or relationships at work.

  • Your identity is kept strictly confidential throughout the process.
  • You are protected by law — both in the UAE and under EU regulation.
  • Your report is reviewed only by a dedicated, independent team.
  • Good faith reporting is always protected, even if the facts are unclear.
EU Whistleblower Protection Directive (2019/1937)
Guarantees confidentiality and prohibits retaliation across EU member states
UAE Federal Law No. 4 of 2016
Establishes reporter confidentiality obligations within regulated entities
U.S. Whistleblower Protection Program (DOL)
Federal protections for employees reporting violations in good faith
